Privacy Policy — BloqOut

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. In short

  • No account, no server of our own: your blocks, stats and settings stay on your phone.
  • The camera analyses your movements on your phone: no image is ever sent.
  • A few outside services receive data: advertising (free version), subscriptions, and AI for some questions and the Coach. Details below.

2. Data controller

The company that publishes BloqOut, named on the Legal information page. Contact for any question about your data: contact@bloqout.com.

3. Data that stays on your phone

This data is never sent to us. It is deleted if you uninstall the App:

  • your blocks (chosen apps or websites, challenge, durations), schedules and running blocks;
  • your stats, levels, records and training programs;
  • your Coach program (goal, level, days, and the free-text "wishes and limitations");
  • your language levels and words to review;
  • your settings (language, theme, Focus mode, Parent Mode and its code, stored as a hash, never in clear);
  • the list of your purchases as returned by Apple or Google.

On iPhone, the apps chosen in Screen Time are opaque tokens provided by Apple: BloqOut never sees their names or your screen time. On Android, the list of installed apps is only used, on your phone, to let you choose what to block. The backup (Settings › Backup) creates a file that you save wherever you want: we do not receive it.

4. Camera, sensors and permissions

  • Camera: during a physical challenge, images are analysed on your phone to count reps and deleted right away. On the last rep, a photo may be taken for the victory card: it stays on your phone and is only shared if you choose to share the card.
  • Motion sensor / pedometer: for walking challenges and the vibration counting mode. Nothing is sent.
  • Screen Time (iPhone) and usage access + display over other apps (Android): to block what you choose. Nothing is sent.
  • "BloqOut – sites" accessibility service (Android, optional): only reads browsers' address bar to block the websites you chose. Nothing is read while you type, nothing is stored or sent.
  • Notifications: reminders and end of unlocks.
  • Ad tracking (iPhone, optional): only for more relevant ads; refusing removes no feature.

5. Data sent to other services

Mistral AI (France) — riddles, brain teasers, languages, Coach

To generate some questions and Coach plans, the App sends Mistral AI: the App language, the language you learn, the chosen theme and level, questions already asked (to avoid repeats) and, for the Coach, your goal, level, days, records and the free-text "wishes and limitations". No name, email or device identifier is sent. Do not write precise medical information or anything that identifies you in the free text. Maths, sequences, anagrams and general knowledge never use AI. → Mistral AI policy

Google AdMob (Google) — ads, free version only

AdMob may process the device advertising ID (if you allow it), the IP address (including an approximate location), your interactions with ads and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Before any ad, the App explains how it works, then shows Google's consent choice (in the EU, UK and Switzerland) and, on iPhone, Apple's tracking request. If you refuse, you see non-personalised ads. Subscribers see no ads. You can change your choice at any time in BloqOut's Settings. → Google policy · How Google uses data from partner apps

RevenueCat (United States) — subscriptions

RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase identifier and your purchase history (provided by Apple or Google) to know which features are unlocked. No banking data goes through BloqOut. → RevenueCat policy

Apple and Google — payments and stores

Payments are processed by Apple or Google under their own policies.

bloqout.com website (hosted by GitHub) — settings and time

The App downloads a small settings file and reads the internet time from our website, so it works even if the phone's clock is changed. As with any website, the host (GitHub) sees the request's IP address. No data about you is sent.

6. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Running the App and subscriptions (performance of the contract): RevenueCat, Apple, Google, Mistral AI when you use the features concerned.
  • Personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (BloqOut Settings, phone settings).
  • Non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding the free App.

Retention: data on your phone stays as long as the App is installed. Each outside service keeps data under its own policy (links above). Transfers outside the EU: Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

7. Children and Parent Mode

  • Parent Mode: when a parent turns it on on their child's phone, ads become child-directed (non-personalised, suitable for all ages), and the App never asks for tracking permission.
  • Age of digital consent: in the EU it ranges from 13 to 16 depending on the country (15 in France). Below it, ask a parent before accepting personalised ads, or refuse them: the App works the same.
  • United States (COPPA): children under 13 use BloqOut only with a parent, in Parent Mode. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 beyond what is needed to run the App. A parent may contact us with any question.
  • India: users under 18 use BloqOut with a parent's consent.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for access, correction or deletion of your data, object to or restrict processing, ask for portability and withdraw your consent. Since almost everything stays on your phone, the simplest is often to delete a block or uninstall the App. For anything else, write to contact@bloqout.com; we reply within one month. You can also contact your country's data protection authority.

9. Depending on your country

  • European Union / EEA: GDPR. In France, complaints to the CNIL.
  • United Kingdom: UK GDPR; authority: ICO.
  • Switzerland: Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP); authority: FDPIC.
  • United States, including California: we do not sell your data for money. Sharing with advertising (AdMob) may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising: you can opt out (Apple's tracking choice, your phone's ad settings, BloqOut Settings) and write to us to exercise your rights (access, deletion, correction, opting out of sharing). We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.
  • Canada, including Quebec: PIPEDA and Law 25; you can exercise your rights at the address above.
  • Brazil: LGPD; authority: ANPD.
  • India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; you can write to us with any grievance.
  • Australia: Privacy Act 1988; authority: OAIC.
  • Other countries: you have the rights provided by your local law.

10. Security

Exchanges with outside services are encrypted (HTTPS). Data on your phone is protected by your phone's security: keep a lock code and an up-to-date system.

11. Changes

We may change this policy. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the App. The update date is shown at the top.
