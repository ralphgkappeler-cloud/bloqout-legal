4. Health and safety — read before any physical challenge

  • BloqOut is not medical advice and not a medical device. Challenges, programs (100 push-ups, 200 squats, 50 burpees, plank) and Coach plans are general suggestions, not a prescription suited to your health.
  • Ask a doctor before starting if you have a health condition (heart, blood pressure, asthma, back, joints…), an injury, if you are pregnant, if you are returning to exercise after a long break, or if you have any doubt.
  • Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, chest pain or feel unwell. No block is worth an injury: you can always give up a challenge.
  • Choose a suitable level. You set the number of reps: start small and warm up.
  • Exercise in a safe place: stable, non-slippery floor, clear space around you, away from stairs, furniture and fragile objects. Place the phone securely.
  • Never while driving, walking in the street or in any situation that needs your attention. During a walking challenge, watch where you are going.
  • Children: an adult must supervise a child's exercise and choose amounts suited to their age.
  • Camera counting can be wrong (light, clothing, angle). Do not strain to get a rep counted.
